# 💡 BoDiGi™ — The All-In-One AI Small Business Builder

Welcome to **BoDiGi™**, created by **Bobbie Jo Gray** — founder of **Bobbie Digital**.  `  
BoDiGi makes *going digital easy* for small business owners by combining AI-powered branding, MVP creation, and marketing automation — all guided by Aura™ (AI Brand Expert) and Boltz™ (AI Product Builder).

---

## 🚀 What’s Inside

| Module | Description |
|--------|--------------|
| 🧠 **Brand Builder (Aura)** | Chat with Aura to design your brand’s name, slogan, colors, and personality. |
| ⚙️ **MVP Creator (Boltz)** | Let Boltz help you build a working MVP idea complete with monetization plans. |
| 🔁 **Learn & Earn Loop** | Gamified marketing funnels that reward customers with free features. |
| 📈 **Marketing Hub** | Learn how to promote, price, and grow your new business. |
| 🛡️ **Legal Protection** | Auto-generates terms and agreements to protect your brand. |

---

## 🧩 Tech Stack

- **Vite + React + TailwindCSS**
- **React Query** for async state management  
- **Lucide-React** icons  
- **Framer Motion** for animations  
- **Base44 API (optional)** for AI and database integrations
